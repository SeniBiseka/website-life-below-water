TAKE ACTION

REDUCE MARINE POLLUTION


A significant portion of the waste we generate on land eventually finds its way into the oceans. Use biodegradable items in our day-to-day activities and never buy bottled water; instead boil, filter, chlorine or use rainwater to meet your water needs.

SMALL ACTION


Make a difference by taking three pieces of rubbish with you when leaving the beach. Just like the Butterfly effect, our seemingly small efforts in conserving marine life can trigger substantial changes.

SUPPORT SMALL SCALE FISHERS


Buy local and certified fish. You can support small-scale producers by shopping in local markets and shops.

JOIN WITH US


Purchase our merchandise, and you can help us raise funds to organize campaigns and cleanup projects to remove plastic waste from the seaside and oceans.
